Version references checked against the official sources below on **November 1, 2026**. Exam availability changes; use AWS's current certification pages before registering.

## Cloud operations SOA-C02 to SOA-C03

The [15-module operations sequence](certifications/soa-c02-sysops-administrator-associate/README.md) was written for **SysOps Administrator Associate SOA-C02**. AWS ended SOA-C02 delivery on September 29, 2025 and began **CloudOps Engineer Associate SOA-C03** on September 30, 2025.

The material remains available for studying operations concepts. It has not been remapped or reviewed as a complete SOA-C03 course. The older folder and file names are retained so existing links remain valid.

Sources: [AWS's version comparison](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/sysops-administrator-associate-03/sysops-administrator-associate-03-comparison.html), [CloudOps certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloudops-engineer-associate/).

## Machine learning MLA-C01 and MLA-C02

The [machine-learning sequence](certifications/mla-c01-machine-learning-engineer-associate/README.md) currently contains **four MLA-C01 modules**. The other eight outline entries are planned, not available lessons.

AWS ended English delivery of MLA-C01 on September 28, 2026. Its updated MLA-C02 beta began delivery on September 29, 2026. AWS states that MLA-C01 remains available in Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese during the beta period. Check the current page for language and general-availability changes.

The four existing modules can support study of their named concepts. They have not been mapped to the updated exam; the folder name must not be interpreted as a current English exam-preparation recommendation.

Sources: [AWS's MLA-C02 update announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/training-and-certification/updates-to-aws-certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate-mla-c02/), [Machine Learning Engineer certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate/).

## Solutions Architect Professional SAP-C02 and SAP-C03

The [16-module professional architecture sequence](certifications/sap-c02-solutions-architect-professional/README.md) is labeled **SAP-C02**. AWS's exam-guide directory states that SAP-C03 registration opened on October 27, 2026 and the final day to take SAP-C02 is November 16, 2026.

The existing material has not been reviewed or remapped as a SAP-C03 course. Keep the version distinction visible when planning study after the transition.

Source: [official AWS exam-guide directory](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/examguides/).

## Other available sequences

The repository also has material labeled CLF-C02 and SAA-C03. Their availability counts describe the repository's files. They do not establish that all lessons have been checked against every revision to an exam guide. Consult the [official AWS exam-guide directory](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/examguides/) for the current objectives and version.

## Future subjects

Only learning paths containing actual materials appear in the main catalog. A proposed subject is not a course until its material is available. In particular, the older broad roadmap should not be interpreted as a current list of AWS certifications. AWS retired the Machine Learning Specialty exam on March 31, 2026; see [its certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-specialty/).

When updating an exam sequence, document the new objectives, review the coverage, and explicitly label the result. Do not rename an old exam folder to imply a completed update.
