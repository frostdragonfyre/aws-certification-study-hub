# Exam Strategy

General test-taking and study approach that applies across AWS certifications — how to read scenario questions, how to manage time, how to study efficiently for multiple-choice/multiple-response formats. Not exam-specific content; that belongs in the relevant `certifications/<exam-code>/` folder.

## Contents

- **[reading-scenario-questions.md](reading-scenario-questions.md)** — how AWS scenario questions are actually constructed and how to read them: extracting requirements before looking at the options, spotting the neutralized-factor pattern, recognizing the "technically works but doesn't scale" distractor, the confused pairs that carry most of the exam's difficulty, time management across three passes, and how to use practice questions so they build reasoning instead of recognition.

Most people who fail an AWS exam knew the services. They answered a question the exam wasn't asking. That's a fixable skill, and it's what this folder is for.
