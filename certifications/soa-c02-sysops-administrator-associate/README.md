**Available material:** 15 written modules, 15 quizzes, and 15 slide decks. This is a legacy exam sequence, not a current SOA-C03 preparation course.

This course follows a different angle on the same story world as SAP-C02: Priya Anand, Meridian Retail Group's Cloud Operations Lead, keeps Meridian Loyalty, Café Sol, and Ridgeline Diner Co. running day to day — the systems Sofía's team designed, improved, and migrated in SAP-C02. Where SAP-C02 asks "how should this be designed," SOA-C02 asks "how do you actually operate, monitor, and fix this once it's running." Start at Module 1 and work down the table in order.

## How this relates to SAP-C02

If you're working through [SAP-C02 Solutions Architect – Professional](../sap-c02-solutions-architect-professional/README.md) alongside this course, you'll recognize the same three systems — Meridian Loyalty, Café Sol, and Ridgeline Diner Co. — from the operator's side instead of the architect's. SAP-C02 asks how to design, improve, and migrate a system; SOA-C02 asks how to keep it monitored, scaled, secured, and cost-efficient once it's live. The two courses can be taken independently, but the shared case study is deliberate: the same infrastructure decisions from SAP-C02 show up here as operational realities Priya has to manage.
